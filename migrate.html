Move to a more private, more secure account

We're rolling out a new account system built around usernames instead of email addresses. Migrating takes a couple of minutes and everything you own — coins, transactions, gifts, UGC, friends, chats — comes with you automatically.

  • Your real email stays private
    Your profile and public activity will no longer be tied to the email address you signed up with.
  • Stronger account security
    Separating your login identity from your real email reduces your exposure to phishing and credential-stuffing attacks.
  • Nothing gets left behind
    Coins, purchases, notes, friends, followers, chats and posts are all carried over to your new account for you.
  • Ready for what's next
    Newer features are being built around the username-based system going forward.

Before you continue

A quick summary of what migration means for your account:

  • Your new sign-in email will be your username @users.darkpurpleof.github.io — your old email will no longer work to sign in.
  • All of your data (coins, transactions, gifts, UGC, friends, followers, chats and posts) is copied to the new account automatically.
  • This process cannot be undone once it starts.
  • You'll be signed out and asked to sign back in with your new username when it's finished.
